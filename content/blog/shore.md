## Overview

Shore is an identity built from stone, glass, and one blue sphere — quiet enough to last, specific enough to remember.

## Focus areas

- Material palette with a single accent anchor
- Typographic restraint across print and screen
- Motion rules that never competed with the mark

## Outcome

A system that could stretch across touchpoints without diluting the core gesture.
