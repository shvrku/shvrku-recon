## Overview

Drift reimagined storage as a material surface — glass panes, quiet density, retrieval that trusted search over folder gymnastics.

## Focus areas

- Pane-based browsing with persistent context
- Density that respected small screens and large archives
- Search as the primary navigation model

## Outcome

The product felt calmer than its category without sacrificing power user paths.
