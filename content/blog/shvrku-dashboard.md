## Overview

[shvrku-dashboard](https://shvrku-dashboard.vercel.app) was a personal workspace experiment — weekly timetable, live clock, note insert, and sign-in behind a stark black-and-white UI.

Built as UI and backend practice under the SHVRKU identity: material enough to use daily, quiet enough to stay out of the way.
