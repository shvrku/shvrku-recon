## Overview

Lattice coordinated projects with cards that felt like paper and behaved like software — the work was the grouping, not the chrome.

## Focus areas

- Card metaphors with real drag, stack, and group semantics
- Chrome that stepped back when boards got dense
- States that communicated progress without dashboard noise

## Outcome

Teams organized work in the open; the interface stayed out of the way.
