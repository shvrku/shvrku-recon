## Overview

Umbra is theme infrastructure for products that outgrow a dark-mode toggle — semantic color, motion tokens, and live theme swap without breaking layout.

## Focus areas

- Semantic tokens that map to intent, not palette slots
- Motion tokens with duration and easing contracts
- Live swap that preserves contrast and hierarchy

## Outcome

Teams could re-skin without rewriting components every release.
